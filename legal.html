Privacy & Data Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026 · Lawang Tropical Properties

1. Data we collect

When you contact us — through our forms, WhatsApp, email or phone — we collect the data you choose to share: name, email, phone number and the message or preferences you send us. When you request property materials through the download form, we collect your email address, the page or property you were viewing, the date and time, and your IP address.

2. How we use it

We use your data solely to respond to your enquiry, send you the information or dossiers you request and follow up on curated opportunities. We do not sell your data.

3. Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of your consent and our legitimate interest in responding to your enquiry. You can withdraw consent at any time.

4. Advertising and measurement cookies

With your consent, this website uses the Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.) to measure the performance of our advertising and to show relevant ads to people who have shown interest in our properties. This involves cookies and similar technologies that record which pages you visit on this site.

Where you have submitted an enquiry, we may also share a cryptographically hashed version of your email address with Meta through its Conversions API, so that a conversion can be attributed without transmitting your address in readable form.

None of this runs until you accept. If you decline, or simply ignore the cookie notice, no advertising or measurement technology is loaded at all — only the storage strictly needed to remember your language preference and your cookie choice. You can change your decision at any time from the link in the footer.

5. Sharing

We work with the following providers, which process data on our behalf under a data processing agreement:

  • HighLevel Inc and LeadConnector LLC (the "GoHighLevel" platform), United States — customer relationship management: your enquiry, the call booking and the sales follow-up.
  • Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. — advertising and measurement, only where you have consented.
  • Hostinger International Ltd., European Union — website and email hosting.

Where strictly necessary to progress a transaction, your data may also be shared with notaries or legal partners bound by confidentiality. We do not share your data with anyone else.

6. International transfers

We are an Indonesian company and some of our providers are in the United States, so your data leaves the European Economic Area. The safeguards for each transfer are:

  • To the United States (HighLevel Inc and LeadConnector LLC): both entities are certified and active under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission recognised as providing an adequate level of protection in its decision of 10 July 2023. You can check this yourself on the official list: record no. 4838.
  • To Indonesia (to us): the transfer is necessary to answer the request you make of us and to take steps prior to a possible contract, under Article 49(1)(b) GDPR. Indonesia also has its own personal data protection law (UU PDP No. 27/2022), fully enforceable since October 2024.

7. Retention

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a purchase: up to 24 months from our last contact with you, after which they are deleted from our systems, including the CRM.
  • If we enter into a contract: for the duration of the relationship and afterwards for as long as legal, accounting and tax obligations require.
  • Your cookie choice: up to 12 months, so we do not ask again on every visit.

If you ask for erasure before those periods, we apply it to our providers' systems too.

8. Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your data, object to or restrict its processing, request portability, or withdraw a consent you previously gave, by writing to sales@lawangproperties.com. We answer within one month.

If you are in the European Economic Area and believe we have not handled your request properly, you may lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority — in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.
