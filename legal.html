Privacy & Data Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026 · Lawang Tropical Properties

1. Data we collect

When you contact us — through our forms, WhatsApp, email or phone — we collect the data you choose to share: name, email, phone number and the message or preferences you send us. When you request property materials through the download form, we collect your email address, the page or property you were viewing, the date and time, and your IP address.

2. How we use it

We use your data solely to respond to your enquiry, send you the information or dossiers you request and follow up on curated opportunities. We do not sell your data.

3. Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of your consent and our legitimate interest in responding to your enquiry. You can withdraw consent at any time.

4. Advertising and measurement cookies

With your consent, this website uses the Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.) to measure the performance of our advertising and to show relevant ads to people who have shown interest in our properties. This involves cookies and similar technologies that record which pages you visit on this site.

Where you have submitted an enquiry, we may also share a cryptographically hashed version of your email address with Meta through its Conversions API, so that a conversion can be attributed without transmitting your address in readable form.

None of this runs until you accept. If you decline, or simply ignore the cookie notice, no advertising or measurement technology is loaded at all — only the storage strictly needed to remember your language preference and your cookie choice. You can change your decision at any time from the link in the footer.

5. Sharing

Your data may be shared with our internal advisory team and, where strictly necessary to progress a transaction, with notaries or legal partners bound by confidentiality. We use reputable service providers (e.g. email, messaging and, subject to your consent, advertising measurement) to communicate with you and to understand how our advertising performs.

6. International transfers

We are based in Indonesia. If you contact us from the European Economic Area, your data will be transferred outside the EEA in order to answer you. We rely on your consent and on appropriate safeguards offered by our service providers for these transfers.

7. Retention

We keep your data only as long as needed to serve your enquiry or as required by law, after which it is deleted.

8. Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your data, object to its processing, or withdraw a consent you previously gave, by writing to sales@lawangproperties.com.
