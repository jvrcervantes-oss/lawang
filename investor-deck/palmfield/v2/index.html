Hak Sewa With Notarial Escrow vs. Informal Leasehold

Much of Bali's foreign-facing market runs on private, unregistered lease agreements with no escrow and no notarial oversight. Palm Field is structured as Hak Sewa (long-term Indonesian lease tenure) directly between the landowner and the buyer, with funds held in a notarial escrow account until the agreed conditions are met — not Hak Milik, which Indonesian law reserves for Indonesian citizens.
