Land zoning · RDTR

What the land's colour actually means

Every plot in Indonesia is classified on the official RDTR zoning map. The colour decides whether it's legally buildable — and whether a PBG can be issued at all.

  • Residential

    Yellow

    Areas zoned for housing. Construction is allowed within the approved use.

    ✓ Buildable

    Yellow · what you can build

    A home, within the approved use

    In the RDTR, the detailed zoning plan, yellow marks land for housing. A house can go up once its PBG, Indonesia's building approval, is issued. Running it as a holiday rental needs its own business licence, and whether the zone allows it is checked plot by plot.

  • Dedicated zones

    Tourism / commercial

    Zones set aside for tourism and commercial use.

    ✓ Buildable

    Tourism · what it's for

    Built for hospitality from day one

    These zones are planned for visitors and business: villas run as rentals, boutique hotels, restaurants. Whether a resort or rental business is allowed depends on the zoning rules for that activity, so it is checked before the first drawing, not after the build.

  • Protected agricultural land

    Green

    Building is prohibited and no PBG can be issued.

    ✕ Not buildable

    Green · why we walk away

    Cheap for a reason

    Green marks farmland and open space the plan protects. Rice fields listed as LSD and Bali's subak irrigation land have their own protection, which doesn't always match the colour on the map. A house there generally can't get a PBG, and one built without it faces sanctions up to demolition. On green land, a low price is the risk, not the bargain.

We only acquire and develop land that is correctly zoned for construction — never green / agricultural — and we verify the zoning before anything else.
